PSYCHOLOGY

The Psychology of Procrastination

A research-informed long-form script exploring why we delay tasks and how to build better habits. Structured as Hook → Problem → Science → Tools → Close, with every claim grounded in psychology research.

  • Format: Long-form (YouTube / Course / Podcast)
  • Core insight: Procrastination is often emotion regulation, not laziness
  • Goal: Help viewers understand the why and build better systems
PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT

3 Signs You're Wasting Your Time

A short-form script focused on habits, awareness, and making better use of everyday time. Built for high retention on Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.

  • Format: Short-form (30–60 seconds)
  • Core insight: Awareness is the first step to better time use
  • Goal: Spark instant recognition and drive self-reflection

VOICE & CRAFT

Sample lines from the scripts.

A quick look at the tone and research integration.

PSYCHOLOGY · HOOK

“You don’t procrastinate because you’re lazy. You procrastinate because the task makes you feel something you’d rather not feel — and your brain has gotten very good at protecting you from that feeling.”

PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT · OPENING

“You’re not lazy. You’re just really good at looking busy. Here’s the difference most people never notice…”

PSYCHOLOGY · CLOSING

“Instead of fighting the emotion, name it. Then shrink the task until the emotional cost drops below the cost of delaying. That’s not motivation. That’s design.”
